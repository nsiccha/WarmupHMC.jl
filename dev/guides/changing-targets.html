Changing targets ​

Advance a NUTS chain under successive target densities while choosing whether the kernel remains fixed or continues to adapt.

Fixed kernel ​

Construct a FixedKernelNUTSState from a warm-up checkpoint or from an RNG and position with a scale operator and step size. fixed_kernel_nuts_step evaluates the supplied target at the saved position before taking the transition. Carry its returned state into the next call. The target may change, but its dimension and coordinate frame must remain compatible with the state.

A transition is not a posterior fit or a convergence check. A fixed kernel preserves its scale, step size, and tree-depth limit across transitions. When the target remains fixed and draws should be persisted, stream_mcmc provides a disk-backed alternative. Release its mappings with close_stream after copying needed values and before deleting its files.

Continuous adaptation ​

ContinuousNUTSState and continuous_nuts_step carry decaying position-gradient evidence and update the metric and step size after each transition. Use adapt=false to take a transition with the current kernel held fixed. reposition! changes the saved position; the next transition reevaluates that position under the supplied target.

A saved position outside the new target's support is rejected. Leaving support during a leapfrog trajectory is handled as a divergence. Positions collected under different targets do not form one stationary posterior sample, so a pooled mean or ESS of that sequence does not describe a single posterior.

See the streams and transitions reference for state construction and return fields. The changing Gaussian example executes a sequence of fixed-kernel transitions and shows its positions and diagnostics.
